## Research profile

Research interests include radio resource and interference management, wireless foundation models, agentic AI, Internet of Things, localization, and 5G/6G. Methods listed in the CV include supervised, semi-supervised, and self-supervised learning; reinforcement learning; game-theoretic approaches; and optimization. Programming and ML tools include MATLAB, Python, C++, TensorFlow, Keras, and PyTorch.

## Selected recognition and service

- Elected Secretary of IEEE P1900.8, the standard for training, testing, and evaluating machine-learned spectrum-awareness models (2021–ongoing in the CV).
- Top-five team in the MIT RF Challenge (2021).
- Team member of SCATTER in the DARPA Spectrum Collaboration Challenge; the team won Phase 1 and Phase 2 and reached the championship final (2017–2019).
- Senior Member, IEEE (since June 2017).
- Associate editor, *IEEE Access* (2016–2025) and *Journal of Networks and Computer Applications* (2016–2025).
- Standards contributions include IEEE P1900.8, ETSI Multiple Access techniques, ATIS Generative AI in Telecom, and an ETSI study on AI-agent-based next-generation core networks.
