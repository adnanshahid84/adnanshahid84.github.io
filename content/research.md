My research investigates how artificial intelligence and machine learning can help wireless systems sense their environment, share spectrum, and adapt their operation. I lead the **AI/ML for Wireless** effort at IDLab-iWiNe, Ghent University and imec.

## A connected research vision

**Intelligent wireless systems for society** is the shared goal connecting these research themes. Wireless AI and foundation models provide reusable representations; RF signal intelligence brings spectrum awareness; and Agentic AI turns sensing and reasoning into adaptive network control. O-RAN and RAN optimization, future 6G systems, and integrated terrestrial and non-terrestrial networks bring these capabilities together in open, resilient wireless infrastructure.

## Research themes

### RF signal intelligence

Machine learning for wireless technology recognition, spectrum awareness, RF signal classification and separation, interference detection, and sensing from radio-frequency observations.

### AI/ML for wireless communications

Learning-based approaches for wireless resource management, interference coordination, localization, and reliable operation across heterogeneous networks.

### 5G/6G and RAN optimization

Intelligent control and optimization for current and future mobile networks, including O-RAN, handover management, beam management, RAN functional split architectures, multi-operator environments, and network digital twins.

### Wireless foundation models and Agentic AI

Reusable wireless representations and foundation models, together with agentic AI approaches for reasoning, orchestration, and adaptive network control.

### Non-terrestrial networks

Research on integrating non-terrestrial networks with terrestrial systems, including resilient connectivity and traffic steering across hybrid TN–NTN networks.

### Connected sensing and healthcare

Wireless sensing and localization applications, including ultra-wideband systems, connected healthcare, and edge learning.
