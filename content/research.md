My research investigates how artificial intelligence and machine learning can help wireless systems sense their environment, share spectrum, and adapt their operation. I lead the **AI/ML for Wireless** effort at IDLab-iWiNe, Ghent University and imec.

## Research themes

### RF signal intelligence

Machine learning for wireless technology recognition, spectrum awareness, signal classification, and sensing from radio-frequency observations.

### AI/ML for wireless communications

Learning-based methods for wireless resource management, interference coordination, localization, and reliable operation across heterogeneous networks.

### 5G/6G and RAN optimization

Intelligent control and optimization for current and future mobile networks, including O-RAN, multi-operator environments, and network digital twins.

### Wireless foundation models

Representation learning and foundation-model approaches for recognition, localization, and interference or jammer detection in changing radio environments.

### Connected sensing and non-terrestrial networks

Wireless methods for non-terrestrial connectivity, connected healthcare, and sensing applications, including ultra-wideband localization and edge learning.

## Methods

The CV describes work with supervised, semi-supervised, self-supervised, and reinforcement learning; game-theoretic methods; optimization; and embedded machine learning.
